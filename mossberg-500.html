Mossberg 500

America's favorite pump-action shotgun since 1962

Overview

The Mossberg 500 is one of the best-selling shotguns in America, renowned for its durability, affordability, and reliability. This pump-action shotgun has been in production since 1960.

Key Features

  • Action: Pump/Slide-action mechanism
  • Gauge: 12-gauge, 20-gauge, or .410 bore options
  • Capacity: 5+1 rounds (barrel dependent)
  • Barrels: Interchangeable barrel system
  • Reliability: Proven track record over 60+ years

Why It's Popular

The Mossberg 500's popularity is built on affordability, reliability, and versatility. The interchangeable barrel system allows shooters to quickly switch from hunting to home defense.

Uses

  • Hunting (waterfowl, upland game, turkey)
  • Sport Shooting (clay targets, trap)
  • Home Defense
  • Law Enforcement Training
  • Tactical Applications
